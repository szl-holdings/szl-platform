## What changed, verified from the diffs

### -09 → -10 (2026-11-17) — recording and authority independence

- **Section 5.2:** independent recording at trust level L2+ strengthened from
  SHOULD to **MUST** ("audit records MUST be written by a component
  independent of the agent").
- **Section 3.1:** `principal.authority_ref` MUST be non-null at L2+ and MUST
  reference an authorization artifact **verifiable independently of the
  agent** — a signed WIMSE authorization-evidence record, an AAT
  authorization record (7.3), or a delegation record (7.4) signed by a key
  distinct from the agent's. Normative text: "at L2 and above an agent
  cannot attest to its own authority." A null or self-signed-only
  `authority_ref` at L2+ is **non-conforming at its stated trust level**.
- Stated by the author to be "in response to independent review."
- Note: -10 shipped without an Appendix D changelog entry for this delta
  (Appendix D documents only -00 through -09 in -10; the changelog resumes
  in -11).

### -10 → -11 (2026-11-21) — falsifiable completeness claims

- **New Section 8.5, Completeness Statements:** a recorder or log custodian
  MAY emit a normal chained, signed record asserting that, for a declared
  scope (`agent_ids`, `session_ids`, `from`/`to` bounds) and a declared
  `as_of` instant, the log holds every record it produced. Carries
  `record_count` and `set_digest` — a Merkle root computed with the Section
  6.4 leaf/node rules. Verification = recompute root and count, then verify
  the statement as a record. Absence semantics: absence of a record is
  evidence of non-occurrence **only** within a verified statement's scope
  and before its `as_of`; outside it, "a verifier MUST NOT infer
  non-occurrence." Explicit honesty clause: the statement "is an assertion
  by its signer, not a property the format can make true."
- **New Section 14.7, False Completeness Claims:** a withheld in-scope record
  that later surfaces and chains to the same custodian is cryptographic
  proof the signed claim was false — the statement "becomes evidence
  against its own signer"; custodians with a falsified statement SHOULD be
  treated as untrusted for absence reasoning; RFC 3161 `external_timestamp`
  prevents back-dated claims.
- **Section 7.7:** `heartbeat` and `completeness_statement` added to the
  lifecycle event registry.

Both revisions converge on SZL's fail-closed / evidence-honesty doctrine:
no self-attestation of authority (-10) and no unfalsifiable framing of
absence (-11). The -11 falsifiability model parallels the evidence-honesty
lane tracked in draft-zhang-audit-record-limits and the honest-verification
taxonomy SZL praised in draft-piehl-agent-rule-provenance-00.

## Status of SZL's four requested extensions (all still open)

1. **Normative offline verification — OPEN.** "Offline" still appears only
   in the implementation-status appendix (aat-mcp; bundled offline
   verifier), not as requirements language in the verification sections.
2. **Level-tied anchoring strength — NARROWED, still OPEN.** -10 closed the
   recording/authority half by making recorder and authorization artifact
   independence mandatory at L2+. But RFC 6962§6.4 Merkle batch anchoring,
   SCITT registration, and external timestamp anchoring remain OPTIONAL /
   RECOMMENDED, not tied to trust level. The strongest tamper-evidence
   remains opt-in.
3. **Envelope-carriage profile — OPEN.** DSSE carriage is still a passing
   MAY ("records MAY nonetheless be carried within such envelopes"); no
   registered payload type or carriage profile. Note: the proposed AUDIT
   WG data-model candidate (draft-gilda-audit-agent-audit-record-04) has
   since made the DSSE envelope *the* record format normatively — see the
   watch record of this date. A carriage profile would let the two compose.
4. **Memory governance — OPEN.** In -11, "memory" occurs only for
   weights-in-memory in the hardware-attestation context. No audit-event
   class for writes to and recalls from persistent agent memory. SZL again
   offers the [Memory Covenant](https://github.com/szl-holdings/szl-frontier/tree/main/covenant-spec)
   as running-code reference: deny-by-default governed memory plane, DSSE
   envelopes, hash-chained receipts, BFT witness countersigning, and a
   Lean-verified monotonicity lemma.

## New cross-fertilization opportunity (AUDIT second BoF)

AAT-11 Completeness Statements and a second AUDIT-WG candidate's
envelope-set model are complementary: AAT-11 defines *when absence is
evidence*; the DSSE-set draft (gilda-04 §4.9) defines *how a set of
enveloped records travels* but deliberately adds no set-level signature,
count, or digest. SZL intends to raise, on the reopened audit@ietf.org
list (Dec 3): a signed set-level statement (count + Merkle digest +
scope + as_of, AAT-11 §8.5 shape) over DSSE envelope sets would join the
two cleanly. This is standing-position material (envelopes + chain
digests + offline verification) and slots into the charter's data-model
deliverable.
